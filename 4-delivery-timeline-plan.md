---

## Overview

This timeline plan provides a structured 12-week delivery cadence for an AI use case. It is organized into three phases:

- **Discovery (Weeks 1-3):** Define scope, architecture, governance, and risk.
- **Delivery (Weeks 4-8):** Build, integrate, validate, and pilot the solution.
- **Deployment (Weeks 9-12):** Finalize production readiness, approvals, launch, and hypercare.

---

## Timeline Overview

| Phase | Duration | Main Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Weeks 1-3 | Agreed scope, architecture, governance, and risk controls |
| Delivery | Weeks 4-8 | Built, tested, and business-validated solution |
| Deployment | Weeks 9-12 | Production readiness, approvals, and operational launch |
